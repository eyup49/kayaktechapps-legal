Privacy Policy — QR Toolkit

Developer: KayakTech Apps

Contact: eyupkayak@gmail.com

Last updated: 2026-11-24

QR Toolkit ("the app") scans and creates QR codes. The app works entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

Data the app does not collect

  • The app has no internet permission. It cannot send data to us or to any third party.
  • It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • Android cloud backup is turned off for the app.

Data stored on your device

  • Scan and creation history (QR content, type, time, favorite flag) is stored in a local database on your device so you can review it. Wi-Fi passwords are hidden in the history view. You can delete single entries or clear all history at any time inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes this data.
  • Settings (for example theme and language) are stored locally.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only to scan QR codes live. Camera images are processed on your device and are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Photos you choose: if you scan a QR code from an image, you pick the image yourself through the system picker. It is processed on the device only.

Barcode recognition

Scanning uses a barcode model bundled inside the app. It does not download models and does not send images to any server.

Links and sharing

When you open a scanned link, phone number, email or location, the app hands it to another app on your device (browser, dialer, mail, maps), which handles it under its own privacy policy. When you use Share, the content goes to the app you choose.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date.
