Privacy Policy — Müzikhane

Developer: KayakTech Apps

Contact: eyupkayak@gmail.com

Last updated: 2026-11-24

Müzikhane is an offline music player for audio files stored on your device. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

Data the app does not collect

  • The app does not request internet access and does not send data to us or to any third party.
  • It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services.

Permissions

  • Audio / storage access (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on older Android): used only to find and play music files on your device. Files are read locally and never uploaded.
  • Notifications: used to show playback controls.
  • Foreground service and wake lock: keep music playing while the screen is off.

Data stored on your device

Preferences such as settings, and playback state, are stored locally. Uninstalling the app removes them. Your music files are never modified or copied by the app.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date.
