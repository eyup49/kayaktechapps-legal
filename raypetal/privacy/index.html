Raypetal privacy policy

Raypetal is developed by KayakTech Apps. The game works offline without an account. It does not include advertising or analytics and does not transmit your progress or settings to the developer.

Progress and settings are stored in the app's local storage. Clear the app's data from Android Settings or uninstall the game to remove them.
