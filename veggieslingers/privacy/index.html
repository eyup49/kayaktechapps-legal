Veggie Slingers – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy applies to the mobile game Veggie Slingers developed by KayakTech Apps.

Data we collect

Veggie Slingers does not collect, store or share any personal data. The game does not require an account, sign-in, location, contacts, camera, microphone or an internet connection.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (stars, best scores) and settings (volume, vibration) are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or to third parties. Uninstalling the game deletes this data; you can also erase it with “Reset Progress” in Settings.

Ads and third-party services

The game contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking. There are no in-app purchases.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
