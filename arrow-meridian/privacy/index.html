Arrow Meridian Privacy Policy

Developer: KayakTech Apps. Effective November 26, 2026.

Data collection and sharing

Arrow Meridian works offline without an account. The game does not collect or transmit personal data and does not send gameplay activity to KayakTech Apps or third parties. It contains no ads, tracking or analytics.

Local storage, retention and deletion

The game stores progress and settings in its private local app storage. This allows you to resume a puzzle and keep your preferences. To remove this data, clear the game’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall the game.

Questions
